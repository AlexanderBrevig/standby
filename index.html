Standby

IPSC range commands, a random start beep and a sound-triggered timer for dry fire.

Needs the microphone (shot detection and voice commands) and works best in Chrome on Android. Say “restart” to run a string, “not ready” after “Are you ready?” to hold.

Dry-fire ritual first: no ammunition in the room.
